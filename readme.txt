Chinese Food Picker

The uploaded index.html is the single-file app. It includes 53 dishes and embedded illustrations. Four real-photo references are listed in PHOTO-LINKS.txt; they are external Pexels URLs and may not load in China or offline.

Language / 语言
- Open food_intro_en.txt for the English introduction.
- 打开 food_intro_zh.txt 查看中文介绍。
- The text files are companion content; the app's interface language setting is separate.

The app stores personal preferences and favorites in the browser on that device. It does not sync between users.

Allergy warning: recipe ingredients and preparation vary. Always confirm ingredients and cross-contact risks with the restaurant or cook.
